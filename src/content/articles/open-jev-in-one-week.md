The first week of building Decisio, first published on Medium on 7 October 2026, and how it compared with Jev on JevBench and the Decision Index.

The title and some figures are out of date. On JevBench v1.6.1 (the board's file, October 2026), Decisio 31B scored 71.69 on the composite and Jev 1.13.0 scored 71.49; the two intervals overlap. The Decision Index figures in the article come from our own 0.2.1 self-run. The 31B now has a row on the index itself: 58.09 on the public set and 58.55 on Decision Index 0.3. The [benchmarks page](/benchmarks/) has the current numbers.
